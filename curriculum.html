❓ Common Questions常见问题

IGCSE FAQsIGCSE 常见问答

Is IGCSE recognised in Malaysia for university entry?IGCSE 在马来西亚大学入学中被认可吗？+
Yes! Cambridge IGCSE is widely recognised by Malaysian public and private universities, as well as all major international universities. It is an excellent pathway to higher education locally and globally.是的！剑桥 IGCSE 被马来西亚公立和私立大学以及所有主要国际大学广泛认可。这是在本地和全球接受高等教育的绝佳途径。
What age can my child start IGCSE at Sunrise?我的孩子可以从几岁开始在阳光学府学习 IGCSE？+
We welcome children from age 7. The IGCSE examination is typically taken at ages 15–16, but students begin their Cambridge journey with us as early as the primary stage to build a strong foundation.我们欢迎7岁及以上的孩子。IGCSE 考试通常在15–16岁进行，但学生可以从小学阶段就开始他们的剑桥之旅，以建立坚实的基础。
How many subjects does a student take for IGCSE?学生参加 IGCSE 需要修读几个科目？+
Typically 5–10 subjects, depending on the student's ability and interests. We help families choose the right subject combination to maximise their options for future study.通常5–10个科目，取决于学生的能力和兴趣。我们帮助家庭选择正确的科目组合，以最大化未来学习的选择。
Is instruction in English or Mandarin?教学语言是英语还是普通话？+
The Cambridge IGCSE is taught in English, which is also the examination language. However, we offer bilingual support with Mandarin guidance to help students who need additional language assistance.剑桥 IGCSE 以英语教学，也是考试语言。但我们提供双语支持，以普通话辅导帮助需要额外语言协助的学生。
Does Sunrise provide official Cambridge exam registration?阳光学府提供官方剑桥考试注册吗？+
Yes. We guide families through the Cambridge International Examination registration process and prepare students thoroughly for their examinations. Please contact us for specific details.是的。我们指导家庭完成剑桥国际考试注册流程，并为学生的考试做充分准备。请联系我们了解具体详情。
