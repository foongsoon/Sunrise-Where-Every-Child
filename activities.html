🎉 Life at Sunrise阳光学府的生活

Learning Beyond the Textbook超越课本的学习

We believe great education is about the whole child — not just grades. At Sunrise, students enjoy a vibrant, joyful calendar of enrichment activities that build character, creativity, resilience and community.我们相信优质教育关乎整个孩子——不只是成绩。在阳光学府，学生享有充满活力、快乐的丰富活动日历，培养品格、创造力、韧性和社群意识。

🌈 Enrichment Activities丰富活动

Something for Every Child适合每个孩子的活动

From stage to science lab, from art studio to sports field — our enrichment calendar is designed to help every child discover new passions and grow with joy.从舞台到科学实验室，从艺术工作室到运动场——我们的丰富活动日历旨在帮助每个孩子发现新热情并快乐成长。

🎨

Arts & Crafts美术手工

Painting, sketching, sculpture and mixed media. Students express their inner world with colour, shape and imagination — creativity unleashed!绘画、素描、雕塑和混合媒体。学生用颜色、形状和想象力表达内心世界——释放创造力！

⚽

Sports & Fitness体育健身

Team sports, individual fitness challenges and healthy movement habits that build strength, discipline and team spirit.团队运动、个人健身挑战和健康运动习惯，培养力量、纪律和团队精神。

🔬

STEM ProjectsSTEM 项目

Real-world science experiments, coding challenges and engineering projects that inspire future innovators and problem solvers.现实世界的科学实验、编程挑战和工程项目，激励未来的创新者和问题解决者。

🎭

Drama & Theatre戏剧与剧场

Role play, script writing, stage performance and public speaking — building confidence, empathy and a powerful speaking voice.角色扮演、剧本写作、舞台表演和公众演讲——建立自信、同理心和有力的演讲声音。

🎵

Music & Performance音乐与表演

Rhythm, melody, instruments and group performance — music education that develops discipline, creativity and pure joy.节奏、旋律、乐器和团体表演——音乐教育培养纪律、创造力和纯粹的快乐。

📖

Reading Club阅读俱乐部

A weekly reading journey through fiction, non-fiction and poetry. Students share their thoughts, debate ideas and fall in love with books.每周通过小说、非小说和诗歌的阅读旅程。学生分享想法、辩论观点并爱上书籍。

💻

Digital Literacy数字素养

Responsible tech use, basic coding, digital creativity and online safety — essential skills for the modern world.负责任的技术使用、基本编程、数字创意和网络安全——现代世界的必备技能。

🌿

Nature & Outdoor自然户外

Nature walks, gardening, environmental awareness and outdoor exploration — connecting children to the world beyond the classroom.自然漫步、园艺、环境意识和户外探索——连接孩子与课堂之外的世界。

🤸

Mindfulness & Wellness正念与健康

Breathing exercises, journaling, emotional check-ins and simple yoga. We nurture healthy minds as much as we do healthy bodies.呼吸练习、日记、情绪检查和简单瑜伽。我们像培育健康身体一样培育健康心灵。

🏆

Competitions & Awards比赛与奖励

Inter-class competitions, external events and a regular awards ceremony to celebrate every student's achievements, big or small.班级间比赛、外部活动和定期颁奖典礼，庆祝每位学生的成就，无论大小。

👨‍👩‍👧

Parent-Teacher Conference家长教师会议

Held twice yearly, these meaningful sessions keep parents fully informed and actively involved in their child's learning journey.每年举行两次，这些有意义的会议让家长充分了解并积极参与孩子的学习旅程。

🎊

Annual Celebration Day年度庆典日

Our biggest event of the year! Students showcase their talents, receive awards, perform and celebrate together as the Sunrise family.我们一年中最大的活动！学生展示才华、领取奖项、表演并作为阳光大家庭一起庆祝。

💚 Holistic Development全人发展

Growing the Whole Child培育完整的孩子

At Sunrise, we pay as much attention to how a child feels as to how they perform. Every activity is intentionally designed to support mental, physical and emotional growth.在阳光学府，我们对孩子的感受与表现同样关注。每项活动都经过精心设计，以支持心理、身体和情感成长。

🧠 Mental Wellbeing心理健康

We actively monitor student emotional health and create a safe, supportive space where every child feels heard and valued.我们积极监测学生的情绪健康，创造一个安全、支持性的空间，让每个孩子都感到被倾听和重视。

  • Regular emotional check-ins定期情绪检查
  • Mindfulness sessions正念课程
  • Open-door teacher policy开放式教师政策
  • Bully-free environment无欺凌环境

💪 Physical Growth身体成长

Physical education, active breaks and healthy lifestyle habits are woven into every school day — because a healthy body powers a brilliant mind.体育教育、活动休息和健康生活方式习惯融入每个学校日——因为健康的身体驱动出色的思维。

  • Daily movement breaks每日活动休息
  • Structured PE sessions结构化体育课
  • Nutrition awareness营养意识
  • Outdoor exploration户外探索

❤️ Social & Emotional社交与情感

We help students build meaningful friendships, develop empathy and learn how to navigate the world with kindness and confidence.我们帮助学生建立有意义的友谊，培养同理心，学习如何以善意和自信驾驭世界。

  • Teamwork & collaboration团队合作
  • Leadership opportunities领导机会
  • Community service社区服务
  • Peer mentoring同伴辅导

🌟 Character Building品格培养

Through activities, events and daily interactions, we intentionally build integrity, respect, resilience and a growth mindset in every student.通过活动、活动和日常互动，我们有意在每位学生中培养诚信、尊重、韧性和成长心态。

  • Student award recognition学生奖励认可
  • Growth mindset workshops成长心态工作坊
  • Values-based learning价值观学习
  • Celebration of diversity庆祝多元化
