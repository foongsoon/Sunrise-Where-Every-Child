🎯 Our Programs我们的课程项目

Learning Tailored for Every Stage每个阶段量身定制的学习

From curious young learners to ambitious teens — Sunrise offers three carefully designed programmes that grow with your child and honour their unique learning style.从好奇的小学习者到有抱负的青少年——阳光学府提供三个精心设计的课程项目，与您的孩子共同成长并尊重其独特的学习方式。

🌱 Ages 7–11 · Primary Level7–11岁 · 小学阶段

Cambridge Primary Programme剑桥小学课程

We believe the early years are everything. Our Primary Programme builds genuine excitement for learning through hands-on, inquiry-based activities aligned to the Cambridge Primary framework — so your child enters secondary education confident, curious and ahead.我们相信早期年龄至关重要。我们的小学课程通过与剑桥小学框架一致的动手、探究式活动，建立对学习的真正热情——让您的孩子充满自信、好奇地进入中学教育并领先一步。

📚 English, Maths, Science英语、数学、科学 🎨 Creative Learning创意学习 🤸 Physical Activities体育活动 🧩 Problem Solving解决问题

📖 Literacy Foundation读写基础

Build strong reading, writing and comprehension skills through engaging, age-appropriate materials.通过引人入胜的适龄材料建立强大的阅读、写作和理解能力。

Hands-on experiments and investigations that ignite a passion for science from a young age.动手实验和探究，从小激发对科学的热情。
