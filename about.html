Our Story of Light & Learning我们的光与学习故事

Since 2014, we have dedicated ourselves to creating an education experience where every child discovers their brilliance, finds their voice, and grows with confidence.自2014年起，我们致力于创造一种教育体验，让每个孩子发现自己的才华，找到自己的声音，自信地成长。

📖 Our Story我们的故事

Born from a Passion to Inspire源于激励的热情

Sunrise Education was founded in 2014 with a single powerful belief: that every child deserves an education as extraordinary as they are. Too many talented young learners were being held back by rigid, one-size-fits-all systems that failed to recognise their unique gifts.阳光学府于2014年创立，秉持一个强有力的信念：每个孩子都值得获得与自己一样非凡的教育。太多有才华的年轻学习者被刻板的、一刀切的制度所限制，这些制度未能认识到他们独特的天赋。

We set out to build something different — a warm, structured homeschool environment in the heart of Klang, Selangor, where children could flourish academically and personally, guided by the internationally respected Cambridge IGCSE framework.我们着手建立一些不同的东西——在雪兰莪巴生中心，一个温暖、有结构的居家教育环境，孩子们可以在学术和个人方面蓬勃发展，以国际认可的剑桥 IGCSE 框架为指导。

Today, we are proud to be a trusted name for homeschooling families across Malaysia — offering bilingual education that bridges cultures and opens global doors.今天，我们自豪地成为马来西亚各地居家教育家庭信赖的名字——提供连接文化、打开全球大门的双语教育。

🌟 Mission & Vision使命与愿景

What Drives Everything We Do驱动我们一切行动的力量

🎯

Our Mission我们的使命

To provide every child with a personalised, internationally recognised education that nurtures their unique potential — academically, emotionally and socially — so they grow into confident, compassionate global citizens.为每个孩子提供个性化的、国际认可的教育，在学术、情感和社交方面培育他们的独特潜能——让他们成长为自信、富有同情心的全球公民。

🔭

Our Vision我们的愿景

To be Malaysia's most loved homeschool centre — a place where learning is joyful, every child is seen, and families trust us as their partner in raising the next generation of bright, curious thinkers.成为马来西亚最受喜爱的居家教育中心——一个学习充满快乐、每个孩子都受到重视的地方，家庭信任我们作为培养下一代聪明、好奇思考者的伙伴。
